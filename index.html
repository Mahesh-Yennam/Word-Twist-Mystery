🕵️ DETECTIVE INVESTIGATION HANDBOOK

Welcome to the Bureau of Cryptic Investigations. Here is your protocol:

  • Objective: Form words by rearranging the 6 available letter tiles.
  • Word Lengths: Words must be 3, 4, 5, or 6 letters long.
  • Case Clearance: You must solve at least one 6-letter word to crack the case. Any additional words award high score multipliers!
  • Score Values:
    • 3-Letter Words: 100 points
    • 4-Letter Words: 250 points
    • 5-Letter Words: 500 points
    • 6-Letter Master Key: 1,000 points + Bonus Case Clear Multiplier
  • Shortcuts:
    • Letters (A-Z): Spell words quickly
    • Enter: Submit current word
    • Backspace / Delete: Remove last letter
    • Space / S: Twist / Shuffle rack
    • C: Clear answer tray
    • H: Use Hint to expose a first letter
    • P: Pause game

🔍 Investigation Guidelines & Scoring Protocol

🔑
Master Cipher: You must crack at least one 6-letter word (1,000 Pts) to clear the case file and unlock the next dossier!
🔀
Twist / Shuffle: Press Spacebar or click Twist to 3D flip and rearrange the letters on the rack.
💡
Detective Clues: Press H or click Hint to expose the first letter of an unsolved word in the case grid.
⏱️
Case Clock: In Timed Mode, every remaining second awards +10 bonus points upon solving the master cipher!

Desktop Controls: Type letters A-Z directly on your physical keyboard. Press Enter to submit, Backspace to undo, Space to Twist, C to Clear, and P to Pause.
Mobile Controls: Tap letter tiles into the answer tray, tap placed tiles to return them, or tap Twist / Enter buttons.
